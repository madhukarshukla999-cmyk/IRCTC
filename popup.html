IRCTC login
The password is stored encrypted with your PIN. Unlock it once per browser session; it is kept in memory only and cleared when the browser closes. On the login pop-up the extension fills your user ID and password; you type the captcha and Sign In is clicked for you.
One per line. Berth (LB, MB, UB, SL, SU, WS) and food (V, N, D = no food) are optional. Clicks Continue, waits for you to type the captcha, clicks Pay & Book and sends the UPI request. You approve it in your UPI app.
Search is clicked at the start time on IRCTC's clock (AC 10:00, non-AC 11:00). If Book Now is not ready, availability is refreshed every 2 s for the retry time.
